- [HashTags](HashTags)
	- Delicious used a non-hierarchical classification system in which users could [tag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tag_\(metadata\) "Tag (metadata)") each of their bookmarks with freely chosen [index terms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_term) (generating a kind of [folksonomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folksonomy)). A combined view of everyone's bookmarks with a given tag was available; for instance, the URL [http://delicious.com/tag/wiki](https://web.archive.org/web/20160411044913/http://delicious.com/tag/wiki) displayed all of the most recent links tagged "[wiki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiki "Wiki")". Its collective nature made it possible to view bookmarks added by other users
