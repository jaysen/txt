 - [Agora](https://anagora.org)
- [about Agora](https://flancia.org/agora/)

a free knowledge commons by Flancian. People keep notes in their own tools and Git repos (Markdown, Mycomarkup or Org mode) and register the repo with an Agora. Each note joins the *node* that matches its file name, so following a `[[wikilink]]` shows every contributor's page of that name. Social posts that contain a wikilink, from Mastodon or Bluesky, join nodes too. Closest to PublicTxt's aggregation; the difference is that an Agora aggregates centrally by name, while PublicTxt aggregates locally by follow, one reader at a time.
