Capital city of the province of [Gauteng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauteng) and the largest city in [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa).
In 2022 (the year of the most recent census), the [City of Johannesburg Metropolitan Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Johannesburg_Metropolitan_Municipality) had a population of 4,803,262, and the wider metropolitan area a population of 15,099,422, making it [one of the 100 largest urban areas in the world](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_urban_areas_by_population "List of urban areas by population"). It is also the seat of the country's highest court, the [Constitutional Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constitutional_Court_of_South_Africa). Situated on the mineral-rich [Witwatersrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witwatersrand "Witwatersrand"), the city has long been at the epicentre of the international mineral and gold trade. The richest city in Africa by GDP and private wealth, Johannesburg functions as the economic capital of South Africa.
