Social bookmarking is an online practice where users save, organise, annotate, and share web page links on centralise public or community-driven platforms rather than storing them in a local browser.

Typically **Collaborative Tagging:** Users assign descriptive keywords (tags) to create shared folksonomies instead of strict directories

see also [Folksonomies](Folksonomies.md) and [human computation](human%20computation).
