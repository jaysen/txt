The C2 Wiki, originally named WikiWikiWeb, is the very first [Wiki](Knowledge-Management/Wiki.md) ever created.

## about
• Creator: [Ward Cunningham](../World/People/Ward%20Cunningham.md) launched the site on March 25, 1995.
• Focus: It centers on people, projects, and patterns in software development, acting as an informal history of programming ideas.
• Significance: It introduced the concept of web-based user-editable pages, paving the way for platforms like Wikipedia.
• Status: The classic site transitioned to an indefinite read-only mode in February 2015, though its concepts evolved into newer federated wiki experiments.
