The [Barrow scale](https://www.britannica.com/science/Barrow-scale) classifies technological civilizations by their ability to manipulate matter at increasingly smaller, microdimensional scales rather than capturing large-scale energy. 

Proposed in 1998 by English physicist and cosmologist [John D. Barrow](https://www.centauri-dreams.org/2023/12/12/seti-musings-on-the-barrow-scale/), this reverse or "anti-Kardashev" scale features seven levels designated with minus signs (from Type I-minus to Type Ω-minus).

## The Seven Levels of the Barrow Scale

- **Type I-minus:** Able to manipulate objects on the scale of the species itself (about 1 meter), such as constructing buildings, digging tunnels, and extracting ore. [[1](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GaDcGsMgGO4), [2](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/what-is-the-kardashev-scale)]

- **Type II-minus:** Able to manipulate living things and biology, including organ transplants, genetics, and DNA alteration. [[1](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/what-is-the-kardashev-scale)]

- **Type III-minus:** Able to manipulate molecules and molecular bonds to synthesize advanced materials and chemicals. [[1](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/what-is-the-kardashev-scale)]

- **Type IV-minus:** Able to manipulate individual atoms, achieving mastery over nanotechnology and complex artificial systems. [[1](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/what-is-the-kardashev-scale)]

- **Type V-minus:** Able to manipulate atomic nuclei and the nucleons within them. [[1](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/what-is-the-kardashev-scale)]

- **Type VI-minus:** Able to manipulate elementary particles like quarks and leptons. [[1](https://www.centauri-dreams.org/2023/12/12/seti-musings-on-the-barrow-scale/), [2](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/what-is-the-kardashev-scale)]

- **Type Ω-minus (Omega-minus):** Able to manipulate the fundamental fabric of space and time itself at the smallest quantum or Planck scales. [[1](https://music.youtube.com/podcast/f3RSvkJVUKc), [2](https://www.universetoday.com/articles/what-is-the-kardashev-scale)]
