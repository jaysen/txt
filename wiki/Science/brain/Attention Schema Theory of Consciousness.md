The **Attention Schema Theory (AST)** of [consciousness](Consciousness.md) proposes that subjective awareness is an internal, simplified brain model of its own attentional processes. Developed by Princeton neuroscientist [Michael Graziano](https://grazianolab.princeton.edu/publications/attention-schema-theory-mechanistic-account-subjective-awareness), the theory explains consciousness through a physical and mechanical framework rather than a non-physical essence. [1](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4407481/), [2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_schema_theory), [3](https://grazianolab.princeton.edu/publications/attention-schema-theory-mechanistic-account-subjective-awareness), [4](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2017.00060/full)

## Core Concepts

- **The Body Schema Analogy:** Just as the brain builds a simplified spatial model of the physical body to monitor and coordinate movement, it builds an "attention schema" to track and regulate its focus. [1](https://mapadelaconsciencia.es/en/theory/attention-schema-theory/), [2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_schema_theory)

- **Control Mechanism:** Attention manages competing signals for the brain's limited computing resources. A schematic model of this focus improves top-down control and regulatory efficiency. [1](https://grazianolab.princeton.edu/publications/attention-schema-theory-mechanistic-account-subjective-awareness), [2](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4407481/)

- **The Illusion of a Magical Essence:** Because the internal model is simplified and incomplete, the brain cannot "see" the exact complex neuronal wiring of attention; instead, it interprets the data as a ghostly, non-physical, or magical awareness of holding items in the mind. [1](https://loc.closertotruth.com/theory/graziano-s-attention-schema-theory), [2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_schema_theory)

## Functions and Implications

- **Social Cognition:** By modeling its own attention, the brain can easily compute and attribute awareness to other people, serving as the biological foundation for a "theory of mind". [1](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32497564/), [2](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2017.00060/full)

- **Artificial Intelligence:** AST offers a functional blueprint for engineering artificial consciousness by giving machines an internal schematic model to monitor processing resources and "claim" awareness. [1](https://grazianolab.princeton.edu/publications/attention-schema-theory-foundation-engineering-artificial-consciousness), [2](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/robotics-and-ai/articles/10.3389/frobt.2017.00060/full)
