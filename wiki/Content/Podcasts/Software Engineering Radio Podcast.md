**SE Radio** is a weekly software engineering podcast for professional software developers. Our goal is to be a lasting educational resource, not a newscast. Since 2006, we've been talking to experts from throughout the software engineering universe about the range of topics that matter to working developers. Brought to you by **IEEE Computer Society** and **IEEE Software** magazine.
