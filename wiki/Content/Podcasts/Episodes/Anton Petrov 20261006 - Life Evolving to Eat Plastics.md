Some advances in life adapting to eating plastics. 

![https://youtu.be/oOzTaK143hs](https://youtu.be/oOzTaK143hs)

The latest video from [Anton Petrov](../Anton%20Petrov.md) explores how nature is adapting to the global **plastic pollution crisis**. Scientists are investigating various biological solutions to break down plastic waste, as traditional recycling methods struggle with **chemical complexity** and **economic inefficiency** 
## Key biological discoveries include:
* **Superworms:** Larvae that can survive on a diet of styrofoam (3:00-4:30).
* **Gut Bacteria:** Microbes from both worms and cow stomachs show promise in breaking down plastic polymers (4:30-6:35).
* **Fungi:** Specific species found in the *Amazon*, *Sri Lanka*, and the *Arctic* have developed the ability to digest polyurethane and other plastics, often using enzymes originally designed to break down wood (6:35-10:10).

Despite these fascinating evolutionary adaptations, the video notes that we are still far from a practical, industrial-scale solution, emphasizing that **reducing plastic production** remains the most sustainable path forward (13:30-14:51).
